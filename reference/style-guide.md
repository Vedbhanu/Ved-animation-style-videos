# Chaaya style guide (locked look + editing rules)

Reverse-engineered from @Prctical.Psychology (see reverse-engineering.md), then upgraded. Approved on
2026-10-13 with video 01.

## Locked (same every video)
- **Format**: 1080x1920 vertical, 30 fps. Default 2–3.5 min (ask; sometimes a 30–60 s Short).
- **Voice**: Kokoro `am_puck`, speed 1.12 (~185 wpm), 0.35 s gap between paragraphs. No captions.
- **Hero**: "The Mentor" sprite set — buzz cut, gold aviators, grey raglan henley, jeans, boots. He is the
  viewer's stand-in AND the comedy target (heart eyes, shocked, crying, facepalm…). Original face, never
  named after or branded as any real person.
- **Cast**: semi-realistic comic-illustration sprites in the same style as the hero (AI-generated by the user
  from our prompts, cut out with cutout.py). Reuse existing cast before asking for new characters.
- **Backgrounds**: flat cream world with a per-section colour theme (gold = good/positive, red = bad/danger,
  grey = sad/lonely/never, blue = calm/insight, pink = love/romance, green = options/solutions,
  purple = fantasy/ideals, cream = neutral/lists). Minimal scenery; props are code-drawn flat cartoons.
- **Title card** at the top for the first ~4 s only: question/hook line + red sub-line in brackets.
- **Sound**: Puck voice + comedy SFX on every visual event + quiet plucky score ducked under the voice
  (mood per section). Loudness target: mean ≈ -19.5 dB, peak ≤ -0.2 dB.
- **Ending**: book/lead-magnet card if the user has one ("LINK IN BIO" + bouncing arrow), then a
  follow/part-2 CTA with the hero pointing at the viewer.

## Must change every video (customise to the script)
- The **story shape** follows the script: list, argument, story with cliffhanger, myth/history frame,
  how-to, myth-busting… Never copy video 01's sequence. Only add a story/myth frame if it truly fits.
- **Hook** is written for this script (strongest claim / question / taboo), not a fixed formula.
- **Scenes, metaphors and props** are invented for each beat (what would make THIS sentence visual and funny?).
- Theme colours, transitions and the score's chord moods follow this script's emotional arc.

## Editing rules (the "same kind of editing")
1. **One shot per beat** (≈ one script paragraph or sentence, 2–6 s). Hard rule: something new appears at
   least every ~1.5 s.
2. **Pin every event to a spoken word** with `at()`: pose swaps, pop-ins, slams, impacts, SFX.
3. **Pop-in, never fade**: characters `born=` with squash & stretch; props `pop()`; text `slam()`.
4. **Key word → punch**: the 1–3 most important words of a beat get a camera punch-in and/or a shake +
   impact burst or slam card + a hit SFX (pow/stamp).
5. **Text on screen is sparse**: max 1–4 words per card (labels, stamps, numbers), except list/chant
   moments where text IS the visual (checklists, "repeat after me").
6. **Reactions are the comedy**: cut to a hero face card or pose swap on every emotional turn
   (shocked/jawdrop, heart eyes, cry, facepalm, dizzy, think).
7. **Transitions**: `whip` by default; `iris` for a new chapter/emotional reset; `splat` for bad news;
   `flip` for a twist/contrast; `cut` inside a continuing scene.
8. **Particles reward emotions**: hearts (love), money (asset/cost), confetti (win), rain/storm (sad),
   flames/speed lines (anger), soul/sweat (despair), sparkles + light rays (ideal/reveal).
9. **Counters, meters, lists, numbered cards** for any number, percentage, list or ranking in the script.
10. Leave the bottom ~120 px and top ~250 px mostly clear of important content (platform UI).
