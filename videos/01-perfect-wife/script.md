# Video 01: The Perfect Wife Doesn't Exist (script v2)

Voice: Kokoro **am_puck** · Target: ~3:00

Title card: **Looking for the PERFECT Wife?** / (She Doesn't Exist)

---

Who you choose to marry is the MOST IMPORTANT DECISION you will ever make in your life. And most men get it wrong, because they're looking for a woman who does not exist.

Your life will change forever on the day you marry. And whether it changes for the better or for the worse depends on who you pick.

A good wife is an asset for life. She will serve you in every way, sexually and otherwise. She will nurture your house, create a positive environment so you look forward to coming home after work, and she will be a good mother to the children she gives you.

A bad wife is a hassle and a liability for life. She will make your life hell. She will take far more than she gives, and make you not even want to go home after work. She might even drag you through divorce, and take half of your net worth, plus a good percentage of your future income as alimony and child support.

So this is a decision you make with patience, knowledge, and your eyes open.

A lot of men, especially young men, think in terms of ideals. They have an ideal woman in their head. She holds the same opinions as him, behaves logically, and acts exactly the way he approves of. The problem? Ideals do not exist in the real world. Only a fool makes his decisions based on ideals.

Repeat after me. IDEALS DO NOT MATTER. IDEALS DO NOT EXIST. THE ONLY THINGS THAT MATTER ARE REAL WORLD OPTIONS, AND REAL WORLD CONSEQUENCES.

These men compare every woman they meet with the ideal woman in their head. And every woman falls short, because the ideal does not exist.

If you live in a modern society, and you want a woman who is a virgin. AND can cook. AND is feminine. AND young. AND beautiful. AND from a powerful family. AND wants children. AND is tall. AND loves you with 100% devotion. AND will sacrifice her dreams for you. AND has a high libido. AND doesn't smoke or drink. AND has a very high IQ. AND makes a lot of money. AND is frugal. AND is logical more than emotional…

You have too many ANDs.

The probability that you find her is zero percent. She does not exist. If you decide you will only marry this perfect woman, you will stay unmarried forever.

In real life, you pick from the options you have. So you must PRIORITIZE which traits you value over the others, and understand the real consequences of the traits you pick.

For example, a docile, very shy woman seems like a good idea to many men. But when they marry her, they realize she is completely dependent on them for everything, and can't even handle basic tasks by herself. Something not working around the house? YOU have to call the repairman. She won't be able to do it.

And some things, you can never have.

A woman who does not follow the female mating strategy. ALL women follow it, to the extent they can.

A woman who loves you like your mother did. All romantic love from a woman is conditional. If you don't meet her expectations for long enough, she will start to nag you, and at some point, she will want to leave you.

A woman who values logic over emotion. Women like Ayn Rand exist, but they are extremely rare. More than 99.99% of women value emotion over logic, and you will see it in how they behave and how they talk to you.

You can find a highly intelligent woman. A tall woman. A woman from a powerful family. But if you look for a woman who reasons like a man, loves you unconditionally, or doesn't follow the female mating strategy, you will keep looking for the rest of your life.

I know this is painful, especially if you're young. But ideals do not matter. Real world options matter. Make the best choice from the options in front of you.

Everything in this video comes from the book, The Wife Guide: How to Choose the Right Woman for Marriage, by Harsh Strongman. The link is in my bio.

In the next part, I'll tell you exactly which traits to look for, and which ones to run from. Follow, so you don't miss it.
